## Implementation

- Added `src/internal/dispatcher` local driver and owner-only Unix locald client.
- `DispatchNext` lists durable eligible intents, filters to local targets and locald-supported operations, then claims by stable intent ID under the existing lease transaction.
- The locald request contains only `intent_id`, `request_hash`, and the committed submit ordinal. Request payload and script bytes are never sent by the driver.
- A validated locald `target_authority` response transitions the intent to `accepted`. A known locald rejection transitions to `not_delivered`; transport, malformed, or identity-mismatched replies transition to `uncertain`.
- Remote-target and local `run` intents remain recorded and are not claimed by this local driver. Their assigned remote/queued-job phases remain later work.
