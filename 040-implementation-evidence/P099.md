## Test approach

Exercise `create_session`, `submit_command`, `cancel_command`, and `close_session` at both M-02 boundaries. First inject a one-shot interruption immediately after the Local API has committed the idempotent local intent, before a mailbox response is stored; retry from a fresh processor and verify the original intent identity is reused and the inbox pair is removed. Then fail outbox projection after the response bytes have committed to SQLite; restart with a usable outbox and verify the response is rebuilt byte-for-byte, the stored mutation operation is not invoked again, and the inbox pair is removed.

P143 remains a future gate. No physical power-cut result or approval for a software-crash-only durability claim is implied by P099.
