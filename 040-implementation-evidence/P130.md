## Contract and scope

- Add a reusable shared coordinator that closes admission and dispatch first, waits for in-flight work only up to a configured drain deadline, invokes the normal cancellation hook when the drain deadline expires, then flushes durable event/audit work and closes active streams at resumable cursors.
- Provide a reusable concurrency-safe gate for stopping admission/dispatch and waiting for already admitted work. Process-specific signal handling and concrete Mac/Linux service wiring remain in P131/P132.
- A cancellation or persistence failure must remain visible as an error; no hook may claim a terminal state without the normal runtime confirmation and durable transition. The coordinator does not turn uncertain runtime cleanup into success.
