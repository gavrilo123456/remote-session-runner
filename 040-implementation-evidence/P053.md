## Unresolved assumptions

- The bridge frame requires `resource_id` for mutation operations; the cancel payload's `command_id` and close payload's `session_id` are required and must equal that frame identity so a retry cannot target a different resource.
- Close policy is forwarded exactly when supplied. An omitted policy is left absent so private runnerd applies its documented `graceful` default.
- P053 forwards only cancel/close. Event streaming and the later real SSH forced-command/network gate remain subsequent phases. P143's software-crash-only durability approval remains unresolved.
