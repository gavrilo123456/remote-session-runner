## Planned deliverable and gates

1. Reconcile a process record from a prior adapter/executor incarnation without reattaching its shell.
2. Compare persisted UID/account and generation identity with the live process, quarantine mismatches, and retain capacity on unresolved ownership or cleanup.
3. Exercise a real Ubuntu process through an old adapter and reconcile it through a fresh adapter on the designated host.

Required gates: focused P042 unit and real-Linux process tests, store/execution regression tests, full `make test`, `make vet`, `make smoke`, selected-toolchain formatting and `git diff --check`, and the full race suite. P042 does not claim the full later P-LNX-01 policy/descendant suite, HTTPS/mTLS, SSH-bridge, or two-host Runner application acceptance.

## Implementation results

**Status: PASS**

- Added `LinuxReconciliationResult` and `LinuxProcessAdapter.ReconcileProcess`, which inspect a process from a prior adapter incarnation, compare its persisted UID/account with the live process, reject mismatches, quarantine surviving groups, and refuse shell reattachment after restart or generation change.
- Reconciliation uses bounded TERM/KILL cleanup and sets `CapacityRetained` whenever identity or cleanup remains unconfirmed. It never creates a replacement shell.
- Added `src/internal/runtime/p042_test.go` for a real Ubuntu prior-generation process, forged persisted identity rejection, generation mismatch quarantine, no-reattachment behavior, and cleanup/retained-capacity reporting.
