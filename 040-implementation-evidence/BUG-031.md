  runnable members. Native BSD inspection falls back to read-only

  ESRCH nor EPERM alone becomes absence or zombie proof.

  identity. Joins, PID reuse, foreign/unknown members, denied or incomplete
  observations, overflow and cancellation continue to refuse cleanup. The
  500ms grace is unchanged; no residual process is newly signalled.

  worker's terminal-job return. One sanitized warning contains validated
  Runner IDs/generation, stage/category, UTC observation/deadline, bounded
  counts and at most 16 process identities. Invalid metadata reports
  `diagnostic_unavailable`. Raw errors, argv, scripts, paths, environment,
  command output and credentials are excluded.

serves as authority to signal processes or release capacity. The proof covers
the recorded original shell group; it does not establish confinement of
